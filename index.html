About the pilot

Model extent

Network evidence

The 2020 asset register provides lengths for circuits. Line 39 includes a documented 3.7 km cable section within a single mixed-line equivalent. Circuit 85L connects Macarthur and Nepean at 66 kV and uses an estimated corridor length. Transformer bank numbers identify the modelled branches. The model assumes that the represented banks are in service on common site buses.

Operating scenarios

Demand is allocated from scaled winter 2020 planning forecasts at 11 bulk supply points. The nine steady-state AC solutions represent synthetic operating points. The external source balances modelled demand and losses.

Reading flow and limits

Arrows follow active power between branch terminals. Thickness tracks active MW. In the inspector, positive terminal P or Q means power enters the branch at that terminal. The voltage screen uses 0.95–1.05 p.u. Published limits retain their original category and season and are displayed for reference.

Geographic view

The map uses the 13-LGA 2020 study footprint and recorded site coordinates. Lines join terminals schematically. Co-located buses are spaced apart for readability. Use the electrical schematic to see the full model, including the Nepean buses and circuit 85L.
